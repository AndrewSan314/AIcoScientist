# Demo Image Source

The `NCM622_CYCLED_453.jpg` to `NCM622_CYCLED_455.jpg` files are SEM images
from the public repository accompanying Oh et al. (2024):

- Dataset and code: https://github.com/MIIMSEKAIST/CNN_for_NCM-composition-and-state-prediction
- Paper: https://doi.org/10.1038/s41524-024-01279-6
- Class: NCM622, cycled
- Imaging metadata in filenames: 20 kV, secondary electron, high vacuum, 500x
- Repository license: MIT

Only three source images are downloaded for pipeline demonstration. They are
not sufficient for scientific model training or performance claims.
