# 3D asset and rights manifest

Reviewed 2026-09-21. Counts below are read from the checked-in GLB accessors; they are not estimates.

| Runtime asset | Source | Geometry | Role |
|---|---|---:|---|
| `presentation/frontend/public/models/calender.glb` | `presentation/assets/calender.blend` | 28,396 triangles; 159 nodes; 159 primitives; 8 materials | Open portal calender with stepped rolls and shafts, bearing chocks, fasteners, loading rams, guides, HMI and service cabinets. |
| `presentation/frontend/public/models/coater.glb` | `presentation/assets/coater.blend` | 21,764 triangles; 128 nodes; 128 primitives; 8 materials | Supported slot-die coater with backing roll, die lips, adjustment carriage, feed hose, web path and controls. |

Both models are generated by `presentation/assets/build_exhibition.py`. The script, editable `.blend` files and exported `.glb` files are retained so geometry is reproducible and reviewable. `ManufacturingWorld.ts` loads the GLBs locally and resolves named rolls for runtime animation. No external model, texture, HDRI or manufacturer CAD asset is embedded.

The models are original interpretive exhibition artwork produced for this repository. They are schematic equipment, not dimensional reconstructions of either source laboratory, certified digital twins or engineering drawings. The repository currently has no `LICENSE`/`COPYING` file, so external redistribution rights remain unspecified until the project owner adds one. Blender was the authoring/export tool; no Blender program code is bundled into the assets.

The microstructure is generated at runtime by `ElectrodeMicrostructure.ts`: 175 layered lamellar graphite particles or 105 textured NMC secondary granules, a fixed collector and sparse illustrative binder bridges. Motion is deterministic, reversible and constrained to disjoint envelopes. It is an authored scientific illustration, not tomography, DEM, pore-network reconstruction or a validated compression solver.

The optimization view uses runtime geometry from checked-in source artifacts. It deliberately has no fitted response surface: unknown candidates share a neutral baseline until acquisition reveals their recorded outcome.

Measured browser statistics in `docs/exhibition/PERFORMANCE_QA.json` are for Scene 4 only: 35 draw calls and 18,368 rendered triangles at the sampled frame. They must not be read as an all-scene maximum. The two GLB triangle counts above describe asset geometry; renderer totals depend on the visible scene and frustum.
