## Scene findings
1. Hero: equipment has almost black reflective surfaces without an environment; a large card and empty gray background dominate. Unsupported acceleration and physics language overstates the demonstration.
2. Manufacturing: closed rectangular bearing supports hide the actual rollers. Coater lacks structural support and readable feed mechanics. Straight foil crosses the backing roll incorrectly. Oversized floating rings and floor grid obscure the industrial scale. Close views face roller ends rather than the working nip.
3. Electrode: randomly intersecting particles float above an overthick collector. Morph frames barely change their crowded silhouette. Binder lines look arbitrary. Slider-derived porosity and thickness are not measurements.
4. Optimization: invented exponential surface looks quantitative. Unknown outcomes already determine candidate height. Dense side panels leave little room for the graph.
5. Evidence: machinery adds little behind oversized cards. Prior report asserts performance without a recorded measurement protocol. Provenance has an invalid Warwick DOI and requires license verification.

## Three priorities
1. Author two detailed GLB machines: stepped rollers and shafts, open portal frames, bearing chocks, loading screws, connected guides, die lips and adjustment carriage, fasteners, cabinets and supported web.
2. Light metallic surfaces with a studio reflection environment and frame mechanisms from an oblique operating-side view.
3. Replace random particles with constrained authored layers, distinct lamellar graphite and granular NMC, a visible collector edge, and repeatable reversible pore closure.

## Interaction and transition defects
Visibility changes immediately while camera motion continues. Two effects issue competing camera transitions on entry to manufacturing. Empty-dependency event handlers retain the initial scene, disabling later drag/candidate interaction. UI swaps immediately. Existing QA silently skips absent controls, and does not prove its intended state. No reduced-motion treatment or recorded motion evidence establishes the previous seamlessness claim.

## Replacement pipeline
Use scripted Blender authoring with bevels, weighted normals, lathed stepped mechanical profiles, and PBR materials. Export GLB and retain the authoring script, source blend and rights manifest. Original interpretive models are schematic equipment, not manufacturer CAD or a reconstruction of the source laboratories. Use Three.js PMREM studio reflections, one shadow key and restrained fill. No bloom dependency. Constrain particle trajectories geometrically rather than suggesting a pore-scale solver.
